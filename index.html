Trust and transparency

Clear labels, honest promises

Verification you can read

Status labels such as Registered, Identity Verified, Organisation Verified and Project Verified each say exactly what has been checked.

Being listed does not mean Little Drops recommends a person or project, and we say so.

Paid publicity is labelled

Paying for visibility never decides whether a genuine need is reviewed, and sponsored content is always marked.
